Ceres Expense Bills Management

Separate Expense Bills from Vendor Bills in Odoo 18

Built for Odoo 18 Community Edition and fully compatible with Odoo 18 Enterprise. Bills generated through the standard Expenses workflow are identified, managed under Expenses > Expense Bills, and excluded from Accounting > Vendor Bills. Category filtering, grouping, posting, payments, and reconciliation remain fully standard.

Odoo 18 Community Edition Enterprise Compatible Multi-Company

Business Problem

By default, accounting records created from employee expenses may appear together with normal supplier/vendor bills under Accounting. This makes it difficult to identify expense-related bills, manage reimbursements, analyze expenses separately, and keep vendor bill management clean.

Solution

Identifies bills via standard expense_sheet_id on account.move (Odoo 18 Expense Reports)
Provides Expenses > Expense Bills with category filters and grouping
Keeps Vendor Bills clean for supplier invoices only
Preserves posting, payment, reconciliation, and reporting workflows

Screenshots

Real screenshots from Odoo 18 Community Edition after installing this module on a live database.

1. Expense Bills Menu in Expenses App

New Expense Bills menu appears under Expenses, between Expense Reports and Reporting.

2. Expense Bills List View

Posted expense bills with Expense Categories tags, reference, totals, and payment status. Only bills generated from Expense Reports are shown here.

3. Expense Bill Form with Categories

Standard vendor bill form with expense metadata: category tags, employee, Expense Report smart button, invoice lines, and full payment workflow (Register Payment, reconciliation).

4. Expense Category Filters

Filter expense bills by status: All Expense Bills, Draft, Posted, Paid, Not Paid, Partially Paid. Search by expense category using the search bar (dynamic, based on configured expense products).

5. Group By Expense Category

Group expense bills by Expense Category, Employee, Status, Bill Date, or Payment Status for analysis. Multi-category bills appear under each relevant category group.

6. Vendor Bills Without Expense Bills

Standard Accounting > Vendors > Bills list shows only supplier/vendor invoices. Expense reimbursement bills are excluded automatically.

Technical Highlights

  • Built for Odoo 18 Community Edition, fully compatible with Enterprise
  • Depends only on Community modules: hr_expense, account
  • Extends account.move only (no duplicate accounting model)
  • Uses standard expense_sheet_id from Odoo 18 hr_expense
  • Stored is_expense_bill with database index for fast domains
  • Many2many expense_category_ids for multi-category bills
  • No Odoo core modifications

Compatibility

Odoo Version: Odoo 18 Community Edition (Enterprise compatible)

Dependencies: Expenses (hr_expense), Accounting (account) - Community modules only

Enterprise: No Enterprise-only apps required. Works on Enterprise installations using the same standard Expenses and Accounting apps.
